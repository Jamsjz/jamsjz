Differential Equations are equations that have [[Fleeting/Derivative of a Function\|derivatives]] of some [[Function]] in them. 
- If the derivatives are [[Total Derivative of a Function\|total derivatives]], then [[Permanent/Differential Equation\|D.E]] is called [[Fleeting/Ordinary Differential Equation\|ordinary differential equation]].
- If the derivatives are [[Partial Derivative of a Function\|partial derivatives]], then the [[Permanent/Differential Equation\|D.E]] is called [[Partial Differential Equation]]. 
- Else, the [[Permanent/Differential Equation\|D.E]] is a [[Mixed Differential Equation]]

**If there is a change that can be modeled, [[Permanent/Differential Equation\|D.E]] is your friend.** The [[Application of Differential Equations]] revolve around modelling change in a system. This property emerges from the [[Fleeting/Derivative of a Function\|derivative]] that we see in [[Permanent/Differential Equation\|D.E]]s.
