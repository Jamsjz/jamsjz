> [!definition] Ordinary Differential Equations
> A differential equation of [[Order of a Differential Equation\|Order]] $n$ is any equation of the form:
> $$
> f(x, t, x', x'', x''', \dots, x^{\left\{ i \right\} }, \dots, x^{\left\{ n \right\} }) = 0
> $$
> where, $x^{\left\{ i \right\}}=\frac{d^{i}}{dt^{i}}x$ is called an [[Permanent/Ordinary Differential Equation]].

**If there is a change that can be modeled, [[Permanent/Differential Equation\|D.E]] is your friend.** The [[Application of Ordinary Differential Equations]] revolve around modelling change in a system. This property emerges from the [[Permanent/Derivative of a Function\|derivative]] that we see in [[Permanent/Differential Equation\|D.E]]s.
